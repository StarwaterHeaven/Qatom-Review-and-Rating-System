## Notes

- A grade measures how well the **listing** is built. It is not an endorsement of the service behind it, and it is not investment, legal, medical or financial advice.
- Scores are reproducible: `tools/score_stage1.py` with the catalog read-back and the reviewer's judgments.
- The reviewer is operated for Qatom. Items marked first party or listed by a Qatom team member are not arm's length. See [`../policy/disclosure.md`](../policy/disclosure.md).
- The single-calculation listings share one unnamed seller and one set of gaps, so they share a summary.
- Where a security finding could be acted on by someone other than the seller, the detail is withheld here and sent to the seller. The record says so.
- A score is taken at a moment. A listing fixed after the sweep keeps its score until the next sweep, or until the seller asks for a re-score through the feedback item.
