- A grade measures how well the **listing** is built. It is not an endorsement of the service behind it, and it is not investment, legal, medical or financial advice.
- `Seller: not set` scores 0 on seller identity. It is the single most common and most cheaply fixed gap in this sweep.

- Thirty-seven single-calculation listings share one unnamed seller and one set of gaps, so they share a summary. They are not a copy-paste error.
- Where a security finding could be acted on by someone other than the seller, the detail is withheld here and sent to the seller. The record says so.
- A score is taken at a moment. A listing fixed after the sweep keeps its score until the next one, or until the seller appeals and asks for a re-score.
