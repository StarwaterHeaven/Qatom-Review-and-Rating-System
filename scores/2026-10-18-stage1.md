## Notes

- A grade measures how well the **listing** is built. It is not an endorsement of the service behind it, and it is not investment, legal, medical or financial advice.
- Scores are reproducible: `tools/score_stage1.py` (with `--delta` for new items) and `tools/merge_sweeps.py`.
- The reviewer is operated for Qatom. Items whose seller is a Qatom partner carry a disclosure line, and are scored by the same code as every other item. See [`../policy/disclosure.md`](../policy/disclosure.md).
- A score is taken at a moment. A listing fixed after it was scored keeps its score until the next full sweep, or until the seller asks for a re-score through the feedback item.
